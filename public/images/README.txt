================================================================================
Swaad Smriti — Archival Images Guide
================================================================================

All food photography is organized by dish in the subfolder:
  public/images/dishes/<dish-id>/

Available dish folders:
- public/images/dishes/hyderabadi-biryani/
- public/images/dishes/mysore-pak/
- public/images/dishes/ragi-mudde/
- public/images/dishes/dharwad-pedha/
- public/images/dishes/dal-baati-churma/
- public/images/dishes/pongal/
- public/images/dishes/medu-vada/
- public/images/dishes/ghewar/
- public/images/dishes/til-ke-laddu/
- public/images/dishes/tekua/

Instructions:
1. Drop your photo (JPEG, PNG, WebP) directly into the relevant dish folder.
2. Register the image in `src/data/dishes.ts` under the dish's `photos` array.
3. Credit team photos as "Source: Team Archive".
4. For internet photos, specify exact photographer credit and source.
5. AI-generated images have been removed and are strictly excluded.
================================================================================
